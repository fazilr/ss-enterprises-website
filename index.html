What we do

CCTV and security services, handled end to end

From the first site visit to cabling, mounting, configuration and after-sales support — one team handles the whole job.

CCTV camera installation

Dome, bullet and PTZ cameras positioned for real coverage of entrances, gates, shop floors and compounds.

DVR / NVR setup

Recorder sizing based on camera count and storage needs, with playback set up on your TV or monitor.

Wi-Fi & IP camera setup

Wireless and network cameras configured for stable, low-lag viewing over your home or shop network.

Access control & door locks

Remote viewing on your phone

Mobile app setup so you can check every camera live from anywhere, with alerts you actually understand.

AMC & repair visits

Camera cleaning, cable checks, recorder health checks and quick repairs under an annual maintenance plan.
